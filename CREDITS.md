# Credits

## This site

**Cyon** ([@Cyon0x](https://github.com/Cyon0x)) designed and built this site.

It was submitted as TASK-15, the DAO Website Navigation Overhaul and Information
Architecture, to the Redbelly DAO Community Task Board. The task ran across both
cycles. It drew four submissions, went to a contested community vote, and was
put to a re-vote before the result was ratified. Cyon's submission won and was
settled on-chain on 4 September 2026.

Everything the site is built on came with that submission: the three pathway
information architecture, the persona-led navigation, the accent system that
gives Developers, DAO and Institutional each their own hue, the component
library, and the written briefs in `features/dao/content-spec.ts` that describe
what belongs in every surface not yet connected to data.

Work done since has been removal rather than addition. The handover shipped with
invented proposals, treasury balances, tasks and community projects presented as
live. Those were taken out and replaced with the briefs describing what belongs
in their place. The structure underneath is Cyon's.

## Attribution policy

Contributors retain credit for their work. Rights in each deliverable were
assigned to Redbelly Network Pty Ltd on payment, under the participation Terms
and Conditions, and every settlement is recorded on Redbelly mainnet. Assignment
of rights does not remove the credit, and no published version of any
deliverable should drop the name of the person who made it.

The full record, including the settlement transaction behind each deliverable,
is held in the `submissions` and `taskboard-provenance` repositories.
